TRAVEL GUIDELINES

Prepare for your trip to the Search iGaming Conference in advance — we have compiled all the important recommendations to make your visit to Cyprus as comfortable as possible.

BEFORE YOU TRAVEL

DOCUMENTS

Make sure you have your passport/ID, travel visa (if required), hotel reservation, flight confirmation, and your conference ticket or registration email. Request VISA Assistance – travel@wintevents.com

Request VISA Assistance - travel@wintevents.com

INSURANCE

We recommend having travel medical insurance valid in Cyprus

BOOKINGS

Double-check your hotel, flights, and any pre-arranged transfers. Keep confirmation emails handy

FINANCIAL ESSENTIALS

Cyprus uses EUR. Most international cards work smoothly, but having a small amount of cash can be useful for local taxis or smaller shops

BEFORE YOU TRAVEL

Transfers

Arrive smoothly using taxis, shuttles, or Bolt from either airport

Registration

Check in at the desk, confirm your ticket, and receive all essentials

Badges

Pick up your badge for access to sessions, expo zones, and networking

Safety

Keep your belongings secure and follow basic on-site guidelines
