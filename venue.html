Venue & Location

Sinergatismou 20-3010, Lemesos 3010, Cyprus

About the Venue

The Warehouse by IT Quarter is a modern event space in the heart of Limassol, designed specifically for tech-driven conferences and large industry gatherings. With its loft architecture, flexible expo area, high capacity, and intuitive layout, it’s the ideal location for the Search iGaming Conference. The venue is equipped with professional lighting, audio systems, and all the technical infrastructure needed for talks, workshops, and networking

How to get there

Public Transport

The venue is easily accessible via central Limassol bus routes, with stops located within a short walking distance

TAXI

Bolt and local taxi services operate reliably. The ride from the city center typically takes 5–10 minutes

From the Airports

  • Larnaca Airport → ~45–55 minutes by taxi
  • Paphos Airport → ~45–60 minutes by taxi

Parking

On-site and nearby parking options include both free and paid spaces. During peak hours, we recommend arriving a bit earlier to secure a convenient spot
