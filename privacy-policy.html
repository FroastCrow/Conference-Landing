Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you interact with the Search iGaming Conference (SIC) website, purchase tickets, or submit your information through our forms.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of data:

  • Personal details: name, email address, phone number, company, job title.
  • Billing information: when purchasing tickets or sponsorship packages.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, and behavior on our website.
  • Communication data: messages, inquiries, or requests submitted through our contact forms

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data. Information is stored securely and accessed only by authorized personnel

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to:

  1. Process ticket purchases and confirmations.
  2. Communicate event updates, schedule changes, and important notifications.
  3. Provide support and respond to your inquiries.
  4. Send marketing materials only with your consent.
  5. Improve the website and event experience

3. Legal Basis for Processing

This Privacy Policy explains how the Search iGaming Conference (SIC) collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website, purchase tickets, or contact us through any of our forms. We may process details such as your name, email, phone number, company, billing information, and technical data related to your device and browsing behavior. This information is used to issue tickets, communicate important event updates, provide support, and improve your overall experience. We only process personal data based on your consent, contractual necessity, or our legitimate interest in maintaining and enhancing the event

Your information is stored securely, accessed only by authorized personnel, and never sold to unrelated third parties. In certain cases, data may be shared with trusted service providers involved in ticketing, payment processing, or event logistics — all of whom follow strict data protection standards. You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal data at any time, as well as withdraw your consent. For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at privacy@sic-conference.com or via @SIC_Support on Telegram
